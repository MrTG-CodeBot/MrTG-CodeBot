### ⚡ The Dark Manga Comic 3D Experience

Engineered with high-contrast comic book visuals and modular Three.js systems:
- **Midnight Manga Megalopolis**: 95+ dark silhouette skyscrapers outlined with glowing neon-cyan (`#00f0ff`) and hot-crimson (`#ff2a5f`) wireframes, illuminated rooftop beacons, and rotating 3D holographic Japanese Kanji rings.
- **Hero Flight & Action Physics**: Your hooded Spider-Man character (white tech hoodie, headphones, red backpack, and Nike Air Jordan 1 Chicagos) punches out against the dark night sky with dual glowing laser webs and streaming comic speed lines.

1. Navigate to: `https://github.com/MrTG-CodeBot/MrTG-CodeBot/settings/pages`
2. Under **Build and deployment** > **Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Select `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder, then save.
4. Your live 3D dark manga studio will be accessible at:
